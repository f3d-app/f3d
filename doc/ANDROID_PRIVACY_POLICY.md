# Privacy Policy (Android application)

## Introduction

F3D is a free and open-source 3D viewer developed by F3D-App Foundation.
This Privacy Policy explains how F3D for Android handles user information.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> F3D is designed to operate entirely offline. We are committed to protecting your privacy and minimizing data collection.

## Data collection

F3D does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

In particular:

- No user accounts or registration are required.
- No personal information is collected.
- No analytics or tracking technologies are used.
- No advertising services are integrated.
- No crash reporting services are integrated.
- No information is transmitted to external servers.

## File processing

F3D allows users to open and view 3D models and other supported files.

All file processing takes place locally on your device. Files opened with F3D are not uploaded to any server or shared with third parties by the application.

F3D does not access files unless explicitly selected or made accessible by the user through Android's file access mechanisms.

## Internet access

F3D operates entirely offline and does not require an Internet connection.

The application does not communicate with external servers or transmit information over the Internet.

## Data storage and security

F3D does not maintain remote databases or collect personal information.

Application settings and other locally generated application data may be stored on your device. This information remains under your control and can be removed by uninstalling the application or clearing its data through Android settings.

## Third-Party services

F3D does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data collection services.

## Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the application or applicable legal requirements.

Any updates will be published at this same URL.
